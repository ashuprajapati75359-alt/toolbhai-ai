Privacy Policy

ToolBhai AI is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection. Browser-based calculators and supported image tools process inputs locally in your browser unless a tool clearly states otherwise.

Some features may use third-party services. For example, the QR generator may request a QR image from an external QR service. If advertising, analytics or third-party APIs are enabled later, those providers may process device, browser, cookie or usage information according to their own policies.

Advertising and cookies

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalize advertising where permitted. Consent controls will be configured where required.

Your choices

You can avoid entering personal or sensitive information into the tools. You may also use browser privacy controls to manage cookies.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact page on this website.
