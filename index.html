How to use ToolBhai AI

🧮 Calculators

Enter your values and tap Calculate. EMI, GST, age and percentage tools run instantly in your browser.

🖼️ Image tools

Choose an image, set quality or dimensions, then download the processed file. Supported processing happens on your device.

📝 Text & QR tools

Count text instantly or turn a link/text into a QR code that can be scanned from another device.

🤖 Smart generators

These starter generators use local templates, so they stay free. A real AI backend can be connected later.

Why this site is useful

ToolBhai AI is designed for people who want quick utility tools without creating an account. The site focuses on common everyday tasks such as loan EMI calculation, GST calculation, image compression, image resizing, word counting, QR code creation and content idea generation.

Most tools are browser-based, which keeps the website fast and reduces unnecessary data collection. The layout is mobile-first so it works well on phones commonly used in India. Hindi + English support makes the interface easier for more users.

FAQ

Is ToolBhai AI free?

Yes. The current browser-based tools are free to use.

Are the AI tools real AI?

The current prompt, title and caption generators are template-based. They do not send your text to a paid AI API.

Yes. Ad slots are prepared. Actual Google ads can be enabled after the site is connected to an approved AdSense account.
