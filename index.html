About ToolBhai AI

ToolBhai AI is a free utility website focused on simple online tools for everyday users. This starter version is designed to be lightweight, mobile-friendly and easy to expand with more tools, articles and monetization later.

Note: The current “smart/AI” generators are template-based and do not call a paid AI API. You can connect a real AI API later.
